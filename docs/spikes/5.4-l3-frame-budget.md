## Pre-registered verdict

L3 is solved in a dedicated Worker; the frame's thread applies the result and updates the layer. Two statistics, both p95 over the real ticker with the software rasteriser off (the rendered window is reported beside it), CPU unthrottled, 200 citizens bodied at once in one bubble on shipped dials, a GC trace running:

- the main-thread window (`apply`, `request`, `CitizensLayer.update`): p95 <= 2 ms, p99 <= 4 ms;
- the worker's own solve time per instant (`StreetLife.solve`, timed inside the worker): the same budget. The margin the story set -- p95 <= 1.6 ms, so the recorded figure plus the tripwire's 25% sits under the budget -- is reported as a risk where it is not there.

Also gated: zero stale frames (a frame drawn with its result still in flight), a result drawn at most one frame after its instant, no worker restarts, every major GC on either thread an incremental-marking finalisation (none in a player's rendered frame or in the worker, the quiet window at most 3 of at most 6.9 ms), allocation under a stamped ceiling on each thread, and each statistic against the recorded finding +25% so L3 cannot get slower unnoticed. The 4x CPU throttle is reported, never gated. Gated by `client/tests/e2e/l3-budget.spec.ts` in `e2e-perf`.

The frame's thread pays 0.50 ms p95 and the worker's own solve is 2.10 ms p95 against the 2 ms budget.

1 of 4 runs on the EPYC 7763 put the worker's solve over 2 ms p95: that is NFR11 missed at the line on the verdict-of-record machine, not flake.

What the run holds against the verdict:

- worker solve p95 2.100 ms over 2 ms

**Risk the next L3 story inherits: the worker's solve has no margin.**

- worker solve p95 2.100 ms has no margin: over 1.6 ms

With no margin, a run on the verdict-of-record machine crosses the budget by a measuring step (0.1 ms) now and then. The absolute budget is the verdict's and not a CI gate (it would go red on a slowest-SKU run in four with no code change); `e2e-perf` holds the recorded ratio +25%, which a real regression crosses.

NFR16 (egress): L3 is client-simulated, about 2.4 GB/month, against about 520-1,040 GB/month with assigned ownership, about 400x.
