## The hot spot, before and after

The first measurement (win32, Intel(R) Core(TM) i9-14900KF, a local run) missed: L3 p95 10.60 ms (p99 11.00 ms), 5035 KB per frame allocated, 1 major GC. The CPU profile put the wall scan of avoidance (`#scanWalls`, a `bigint` chunk key per tile lookup) first by a wide margin. Fixes, none touching a dial, a count or a threshold: the collision grid memoises its last chunk, avoidance shares one wall scan per cell and grid revision, and the per-body per-frame allocations of the hot path were removed (boxed doubles across calls, comparator and sort scratch, a key string and a point object per body per frame).
