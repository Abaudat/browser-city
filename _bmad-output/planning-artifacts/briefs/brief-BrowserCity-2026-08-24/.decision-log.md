### Core fantasy resolved

- **DECISION: Core fantasy is #1 "One part of a massive thing"** — significance without centrality. Chosen over #2 (The Breathing Machine) and #3 (Possibility Vertigo), which survive as supporting textures but do not get to decide cuts.
- **CONFLICT SURFACED with prior settled decision:** brainstorm settled "simulation fidelity is the highest-priority pillar, ahead of granularity." Under fantasy #1, fidelity is demoted from an end to a means — it exists to produce economic pressure and social position, not for its own sake. Flagged to Adrian for resolution.
- **Consequence recorded:** cut priority now favours rent/economy/social position over content breadth and chain visibility. Endgame #49 (Becoming A Dependency) is confirmed as the fantasy's payoff, not an add-on.

### Player goals resolved

- **DECISION: Two load-bearing goals besides wealth — (a) Being needed / position (#49), (b) Lateral personal pursuit.** Adrian corrected my framing: discovery is NOT territory-only. It is the whole field of optional non-heroic pursuits — hobbies, golf tournaments, plushy collections, getting good at cooking, places. Territory is one avenue among many.
- **Consequence:** this dissolves the self-termination problem AND the collision with #65 (one district first). A wide lateral activity space renews without needing more map.
- **DEMOTED to texture, not goals:** being known (#97) and competence/mastery (#35/#36). Social cluster (#52 flatshare, #53 group leisure, #75 social glue) reclassified from goals to conditions.
- **DECISION: #106 vs #109 resolved as "diegetic carriers only."** No UI counters for progression; the world carries the evidence.
- **Adrian's stated position on carriers: "nothing, memory is enough"** — accepting an exception to law #38 for places-seen. NOTE: coach flagged that most of his own examples (plushies on a shelf, trophies, food that comes out well) DO have physical carriers; only places-seen genuinely lacks one. Exception is therefore narrow, not general. Pending Adrian's confirmation.

### Core loop, pillars, comparables

- **DECISION: Core loop is the day's time budget.** Minutes are the resource; work converts minutes to money; money buys minutes back; rent takes a bite; the remainder is spent on a life. Written around the daily money-or-a-life choice, not around the commute or the procedure.
- **DECISION: Night shift is a phase of the day loop** — resolved cleanly against #48 (sessions end when real life ends them): the phase is only reached by players still at the keyboard past their character's bedtime. Log off and the phase runs without you (understudy sleeps the character). Not mandatory content.
- **DECISION: Four pillars ship — A, B, D, E.** (A) Time is the only real currency. (B) The city is indifferent. (D) You are always covered / reciprocal occupancy. (E) Procedure over prompt. Coach recommended cutting D to architecture; Adrian kept it — noted as deliberate, D is acknowledged load-bearing for absence, sleep, night population and institutional fault tolerance at once.
- **Law, not pillar:** #38 consequence needs a physical carrier. Stated as a design law in the brief, not as a pillar.
- **DECISION: Comparables are Kenshi / Project Zomboid, Habbo / Eco / Ultima Online, and Space Station 13** (Adrian's addition: "one cog in the machine, low granularity gameplay"). Explicitly NOT taken as comps: Stardew/Animal Crossing, Papers Please/Shenmue — offered and declined.
- **SS13 materially de-risks Design Challenge #1 (the Burger Test)** — it is existing evidence that players voluntarily hold mundane institutional roles for their own sake. Noted for the risks section.

### Scope and MVP

- **Coach raised concern:** designed scope (persistent, always-ticking, browser MMO) vs. capacity (solo, evenings + weekends, indefinite) is the quality bar's classic "scope too large for the team" red flag. Coach recommended a single-player vertical slice MVP.
- **DECISION (Adrian, against coach recommendation): MVP is a small multiplayer district.** Reaffirmed after the concern was stated. Proceeding with full scope; concern recorded here and carried into the brief's risk section.
- **Coach partially retracts the objection:** given #62 (authoritative sim, thin client) is already a committed architecture, multiplayer is close to incrementally free — the server simulates the city with zero clients connected regardless. Building single-player first and retrofitting netcode is the more expensive path, not the cheaper one. The real cost is NOT multiplayer; it is (a) always-on server burn during a multi-year pre-revenue build and (b) content breadth.
- **Capacity: evenings + weekends, indefinite, no deadline.** Scope must survive months of no progress. Brief will state this plainly rather than implying a timeline.
- **DECISION: District one is a full district, 100+ interiors, multiple neighbourhoods, real transit, several institutions.** Chosen over the neighbourhood option. Supersedes the brainstorm's #65 "one neighbourhood, not a metropolis" reading — #65 survives only in the sense that the city expands beyond district one later.
- **DECISION: Five or more playable jobs at first playable.**
- **DECISION: Server burn is "meaningful but bounded"** — Adrian will pay real money monthly for years pre-revenue. Always-ticking (#60/#61) is preserved; the spin-down-when-empty option was offered and declined, so the Truth Test stands.
- **DECISION: Target audience is cozy-persistent players** (Animal Crossing / Habbo lineage — a place to be rather than a game to beat). Explicitly NOT the SS13/Kenshi crowd, and not sim-watchers, though SS13/Kenshi/Eco/UO remain the comparables.
- **CONFLICT SURFACED:** audience (cozy-persistent) vs. comparables and pillars (harsh, systems-first, pressure-heavy). Raised with Adrian for resolution.
- **Scope concern reaffirmed twice by Adrian** (maximal MVP, maximal district, maximal job count, minimal capacity). Coach ceases to argue; recorded as the brief's headline risk with explicit reasoning, per Adrian's decision.
- **DECISION: Tone resolved as "cozy in consequence, harsh in arithmetic."** Minutes stay genuinely scarce; nothing bad is ever irreversible. Reconciles the cozy-persistent audience with the harsh comparables. Derived from Adrian's already-settled safety nets (#72 playable floor, #73 the net is content, #74 night shift floor, #75 social glue, #82 no death) — the design was already cozy underneath; this names it.
- **DECISION: Audio is city ambience with minimal, diegetic music.** Serves pillar B and #9 (UX-minimal diegesis); cheapest option for a solo dev. No composed score committed.
- **Art direction is not a decision** — fixed by the LimeZu Modern Exteriors/Interiors tilesets already in the repo.
