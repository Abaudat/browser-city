### Section: Core Gameplay Loop + Win/Loss — walked 2026-08-25

| # | Decision | Rationale | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| D14 | **In-city day = 60 real minutes.** 1 in-city hour = 2.5 real min. | Resolves brief open question **Q6** (brief gave a 1–2h range). Chosen over 90 min so the loop closes twice per hour of play and the rent metronome is felt. Facilitator's initial concern that a 20-real-minute shift is too short for a discretionary middle was **withdrawn** — 20 min minus ~2.5 min of open/close ritual leaves ~17 real min of duties and unsupervised time, which is ample. | Settled |
| D15 | **Rent falls due weekly** (every 7 in-city days ≈ 7 real hours) | Long enough that a bad day is survivable and recoverable ("nothing bad is irreversible"), short enough to stay felt. Daily rejected as relentless and as making one missed day a crisis, which fights "cozy in consequence". Monthly rejected — rent becomes a distant boulder and the daily loop loses its pressure source. | Settled |
| D16 | **Emergence enters the loop via the commute.** The commute is the player's sensor on the city's state. | Challenge raised: the loop as briefed had **no variance** — day 100 was structurally identical to day 1, and the entire emergence list (rush hour, broken windows, response time as a budget line) sat outside the loop as ambience.
